# MELRA Brand Assets

**MELRA** stands for **Modular Execution Layer for Reliable Autonomy**.

The identity represents modular execution units converging on one durable,
verified path. It should feel precise, calm, technical, and trustworthy—not
anthropomorphic, magical, or speculative.

The official product assets are:

## Visual system

### Mark

The symbol uses interlocking modules and a continuous verified route. Preserve
its proportions and negative space. Do not redraw it, add effects, rotate it,
or combine it with another mark.

### Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon | Near-black foundation | `#0B0D10` |
| Signal | Verified cyan | `#41D9C7` |
| Relay | Modular blue | `#5B7CFA` |
| Proof | Warm evidence accent | `#FFB45E` |
| Paper | Light surface | `#F5F7FA` |
| Slate | Secondary text | `#8D98A8` |

Use Carbon and Paper as dominant surfaces. Signal identifies verified state,
Relay identifies execution flow, and Proof is reserved for evidence or
approval—not decoration.

### Typography

Use a modern grotesk or system sans for product language and a legible
monospace face for commands, identifiers, receipts, and evidence. Do not use
decorative science-fiction display faces.

### Clear space and size

Keep clear space equal to one internal module width on every side. The symbol
must remain at least 24 pixels wide on screen or 8 millimetres in print. Use
the full wordmark when the symbol would be unfamiliar.

### Accessibility

Do not rely on color alone to communicate policy, approval, failure, or
verification. Maintain WCAG AA contrast for text and interface applications.
