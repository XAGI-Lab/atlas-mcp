# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in ATLAS MCP a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give technical claims clear evidence.
- Respect privacy, security embargoes, and responsible disclosure.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading security claims.
- Disrupting project spaces or repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the XAGI
Labs GitHub organization. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue
warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants
when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
retaliation against good-faith reporters.
